THE 2D "MIST PLANE" IN RAIN = RW's wind debris / whirlwind effects (not Vestigia, not the rain particles)

Realistic Weather (RealisticWeather.asi) spawns two wind effects near the player in strong wind:
  - Wind debris  : vanilla wind dust/debris clouds (env_wind_debris_*), drift along the ground
  - Whirlwind    : a dust-swirl column (ent_amb_wind_litter_dust_swirl), vertical and round
In rain these dust textures look like a white 2D mist cloud that follows you.

WHERE: RealisticWeather.ini in your game folder (next to RealisticWeather.asi and RDR2.exe),
section [RealisticWeather]. RW creates it the first time the game runs with the mod.
Edit with Notepad, save, restart the game.

STEP 1 - TEST (turn both off):
    SevereDebrisEnabled=0
    WhirlwindEnabled=0
  If the mist plane is gone -> it was RW. Then try step 2 to find which one, or just leave them off.

STEP 2 - find which one (turn ONE back on at a time):
    SevereDebrisEnabled=1   (WhirlwindEnabled=0)   -> mist back? it's the debris
    WhirlwindEnabled=1      (SevereDebrisEnabled=0) -> mist back? it's the whirlwind

STEP 3 - keep the effect but make it subtle (instead of off):
    Debris     : SevereDebrisAlphaPercent=33  -> 10     (how visible)
                 SevereDebrisScalePercent=85  -> 60     (how big)
                 SevereDebrisFarClipMeters=45 -> 25     (how far away it can appear)
    Whirlwind  : WhirlwindAlphaPercent=40     -> 12
                 WhirlwindScalePercent=80     -> 60
                 WhirlwindChancePercentPerMinute=14 -> 5   (how often)

Default values (to undo): SevereDebrisEnabled=1, SevereDebrisAlphaPercent=33, SevereDebrisScalePercent=85,
SevereDebrisFarClipMeters=45, WhirlwindEnabled=1, WhirlwindChancePercentPerMinute=14,
WhirlwindAlphaPercent=40, WhirlwindScalePercent=80, WhirlwindFarClipMeters=40

Nuclear option: EnhancedWindDynamics=0 turns off ALL of RW's wind features (gusts, debris, whirlwinds)
but keeps RW's weather transitions/persistence.

If you use the RW trainer: it reports when debris / a whirlwind is active, so you can check the
trainer at the moment the mist appears.
