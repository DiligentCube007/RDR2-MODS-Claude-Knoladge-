The white glow on items you can pick up.

These files are YOUR merged visualsettings.dat (Vestigia + RW grass sway + your tuning)
with only this option's lines changed - nothing else is lost.

HOW TO USE: copy visualsettings.dat from ONE option folder to  Vestigia/active/ess  (replace).
'0 - current (installed)' puts back what you have now.

COMBINING with another visualsettings option (e.g. TAA + motion blur): copy one option's file,
then open visualsettings.dat in Notepad and change the lines listed in the other option's
'lines changed.txt' by hand.

Options: 1 - disabled
