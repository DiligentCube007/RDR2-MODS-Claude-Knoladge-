Rain drop visibility presets (only the 5 *_render_drop files; works with the merged Rain Particles Fix rain).
Copy the files of ONE option into the active folder: the top-level files go to active/ess, the str/ files go to active/str.

1 - softer (previous) : drops more see-through; very faint against bright sky and clouds
0 - default (balanced): currently installed. Drops have more of their own colour and are slightly darker than the sky
2 - stronger          : most solid, darkest drops; easiest to see against clouds

Settings changed: particleColorPercentage (own colour vs see-through), directionalAndAmbientMultiplier (drop brightness),
backgroundDistortionVisibilityPercentage / AlphaBooster (how much the drop bends the background).
